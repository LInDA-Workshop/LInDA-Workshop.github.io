<p><i>To be confirmed</i></p><h3>Metalinguism in LLMs: A Hope for Endangered Languages?</h3>
<p>LLMs can use descriptions of a language, such as grammar descriptions and dictionaries, to process it by exploiting their metalinguistic skills. The panel will discuss whether this ability can help languages with very little data, especially endangered languages. Panelists and the audience will discuss what works today, what the limits are, and what the field needs next. The ethical challenges when working with low-resource languages will also be actively considered.</p>
