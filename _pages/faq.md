<h3>Is releasing code and data required?</h3>
<p>We follow the general recommendations from ACL, which is to foster reproducibility and open science. It is heavily recommended to release data and code. However, lab, community, or company policies may restrict publication, in particular regarding data.</p>

<h3>Can independent researchers without an institutional affiliation submit?</h3>
<p>Independent researchers can submit.</p>

<h3>Is a negative result acceptable?</h3>
<p>Negative results are also welcome.</p>

<h3>Do intrinsic evaluations of linguistic knowledge count as demonstrating an impact on NLP?</h3>
<p>Intrinsic evaluation can also count as long as there is in-depth analysis or evaluation of that knowledge in the language model.</p>

<h3>Is a study of a single language acceptable?</h3>
<p>A study on one language is of course welcome.</p>

<h3>Can we use open and closed models in the submission?</h3>
<p>We prefer open models, but works based on closed source models are of course acceptable as well.</p>
